Our story

Paper Boat began with a small room, a few crates of secondhand books and a very stubborn espresso machine. We wanted a place where nobody rushes you: where you can browse, sit by the window and stay for as long as the chapter takes.

Today we stock new and used books in Portuguese, English and a few other languages, and bake something fresh every morning. Come in out of the rain, or the sun, and stay a while.
